 Income tax (India)
  • Tax is on profit, not on sales. Every business cost counts against profit: Supabase, Vercel, AI,
    app-store accounts, payment fees, marketing, devices, design tools.
  • Section 80-IAC: a DPIIT-recognised startup can deduct 100% of profit for any 3 of its first 10
    years. Conditions: incorporated before 1 April 2030; turnover under ₹100 crore; an innovative
    product; a certificate from the Inter-Ministerial Board (DPIIT recognition alone is not enough).

  • Once GST-registered, GST paid on Indian business purchases (such as the GST on Razorpay's
    fees) is claimed back as input tax credit.
  • The CA chooses the company's income-tax regime.

 Never
  • Skip GST registration once it is required.
  • Route money through friends, a personal account or an overseas company to avoid Indian tax.
  • Sell US subscriptions through an Indian gateway alone and ignore US state tax limits.

 AI cost
  • Only Pro users' AI ingredient checks cost money; free users cost nothing for AI.

  • Plan estimate: $0.04-0.05 per Pro user per month at about 3 meals a day, under 1% of a $5.99
    subscription.
  • Beta: 150 users × about $0.05 = about $7 a month, about $14 for the 8-week beta.
  • The regional AI accuracy test (300 typed dishes, 100 photos) is a one-off cost of a few dollars.

 AI spending limit and protections (reinstated 8 November 2026)
  • Why: normal cost is tiny, but a runaway loop, automated abuse, repeated requests, a leaked key
    or a wrong model setting could run up a large bill quickly.
  • Monthly limit: configurable, default $50 in the beta; when reached, AI pauses for everyone and
    meals still save. Alerts to the founder at 50%, 80% and 100%.
  • Per user: 10 AI checks an hour, 40 a day. Size limits on text and photos. One retry only. Same
    meal never sent twice.
  • Emergency kill switch, approved models only, cost-anomaly alert, server-only keys with separate
    test and live keys, and the provider's own spending limit as a second net.
  • Whatever happens to AI, meals always save with ingredients unknown and can be filled in by
    hand.
