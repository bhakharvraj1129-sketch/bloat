 Help page text
     When to get medical help

     Bloat is a tracker. It can't tell you what is causing your symptoms or whether they are serious.

     Call 911 (USA and Canada) or 112 (India) now if you have bloating with a swollen stomach and:
     stomach pain that is severe and came on very suddenly; you are vomiting blood, or your vomit looks
     like coffee grounds; severe difficulty breathing – you're gasping, choking or unable to get words out.

     Contact a doctor today or go to urgent care if you have bloating and: vomiting, diarrhoea or
     constipation; stomach pain; a high temperature, or you feel hot, cold or shivery; a swelling or lump
     in your tummy; you can't pee, poo or pass gas; a burning feeling in your chest from stomach acid
     (acid reflux).

     Book a regular appointment with a doctor if: you feel bloated regularly; you've tried changing
     what you eat and still keep feeling bloated; you have bloating and are losing weight without trying;
     you have bloating and blood in your poo.

     Source: NHS, "Bloating" (quoted word for word; only the emergency number is changed for the
     user's country). This page is general information, not medical advice. [ I've read this ]
 The stored source copy and its date checked live in the evidence file; follow the attribution rule in the licence
 shown at the bottom of the NHS page. A test (Q148) checks the app text matches the stored copy.

 Banned words in the UI and marketing
 safe food, bad food, trigger confirmed, cause of your bloating, cure, heal, diagnose, abnormal, high
 risk, all clear, you may have [condition], doctor approved, clinically proven.

 Incident handling
  • Every Worse card tap, "I felt worse" ending and wellbeing pause appears in a founder review
    queue; reviewed within 7 days.
  • A pattern of harm switches off the card, experiment or feature via its flag immediately, and is
    logged in the evidence file.
  • A safety-related support message is answered by the founder regardless of plan.
