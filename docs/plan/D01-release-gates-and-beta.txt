 Beta plan
  • 25 discovery interviews (guide already written) across the three countries.
  • Early access instead of a usability panel: the first real users simply use the app, and in-app
    counts (no ad trackers, no health data) measure onboarding completion, morning reviews,
    confirmed days, time to first possible trigger, Upgrade taps and Worse / felt-worse taps.
  • 150 adults (50 per country) with at-least-weekly bloating, 8 weeks, Pro free; optional in-app
    2-minute feedback.
  • Pricing test: the Upgrade button shows the real price; a tap shows "Pro is free during the beta"
    and is counted.
