 A16. Daily cards and Learn library (Pro)
 One card a day, stable all day, alternating tip and mind–body activity [FIX 4.3.1]. Each card restates
 one sentence from its cited source in plain words, with the link underneath; no card gives advice
 that isn't in its source. Every card ends: "General wellness information, not medical advice. If you
 have a diagnosed condition, follow your doctor's advice first."

 Rules: triggers before rotation; "Why you're seeing this" states the real reason; 14-day cooldown; when every
 allowed card of that type was shown in the last 14 days, the one shown longest ago is used (never a filler card
 while any allowed card exists); Worse hides and logs; exclusions only from published sources; no card published
 without source sentence, label and version. The 20 cards above are enough for one card a day; the target is 40 for
 variety.
