Model                   General wellness tracker with no outside review of any kind (no clinician, dietitian or
                        lawyer). Safety comes from cautious defaults, word-for-word public sources and
                        swap-only food experiments. Free and Pro plans, USA first with Canada and India in the
                        same launch.

                        record · Part IV: business setup, taxes and fees, legal templates, budget and AI costs.
                        The data files for Claude Code are embedded invisibly in this PDF.

Status                  Specification only. App not built. All tests NOT RUN. v4.3.1 added the six-month-use

                        the 36 gap-audit fixes (section 0.3); v4.5 adds AI protection, Your journey and personal
