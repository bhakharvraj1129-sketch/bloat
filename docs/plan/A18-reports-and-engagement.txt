 Keep an eye on it mode (Pro) [FIX 4.3.1]
  • Offered after 8 weeks of Pro with 30+ confirmed days; anyone can switch it on in Me; one tap
    returns to full logging.
  • Daily: Bloat Score and episodes as normal; morning review adds KM-02 "Had any of these
    yesterday?" for up to 5 food groups chosen in KM-01 (Yes / No / Not sure); followed habits.
  • Meal logging becomes optional. Explicit KM-02 Yes / No answers set exposure for those groups,
    which creates more usable "without" days than full logging.
  • Charts, 7-day average, weekly report, monthly summary, doctor PDF and comparisons for the
    watched groups all continue.

 Engagement rules
  • No push notifications or WhatsApp reminders in this release. Optional opt-in weekly email with a
    neutral link and no health details.
  • Streaks count logging only, never change analysis.
  • No projected "days to insight" or symptom-free competitions.
  • Recent and saved dishes, clear save feedback, Pattern Report as the weekly reason to return.
