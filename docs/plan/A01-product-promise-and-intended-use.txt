 Part A: Product and experience

 A01. Product promise and intended use
 Bloat is an installable mobile web app for adults 18+ to record bloating, meals, habits, bowel
 movements and exercise; see patterns in their own records; run simple experiments; and
 bring a clear report to a doctor.

 Title: Bloat: Bloating and Gut Tracker. Tagline: Track your bloating. Spot your possible
 triggers. Bring real data to your doctor.

 Intended-use statement (fixed text)
    Bloat is a general wellness app for adults. It lets people record bloating, meals, bowel movements,
    exercise and daily habits, and shows patterns in their own records. Bloat does not diagnose, treat,
    cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or condition, does not give medical advice, and is not for
    emergencies.

 Why this design
 With no outside review of any kind, Bloat must stay a general wellness product. The FDA's January
 2026 general wellness guidance says a product loses that status if it is meant to diagnose, treat or
 prevent a condition, guides specific medical action, names a specific condition when suggesting a
 doctor, calls outputs abnormal, uses clinical thresholds or gives ongoing disease-management
 alerts. Apps that triage symptoms (Ada Health, a certified medical device) or claim to treat IBS
 (Parallel, FDA-authorised; Cara Care, certified in Germany) are regulated. Consumer trackers
 (mySymptoms, GutMap, Gutsy, Debloaty) track and show trigger suspects with disclaimers; none
 has warning-sign guidance or eating-disorder safeguards. Bloat does all of this.
