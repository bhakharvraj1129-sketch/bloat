 Part II: How every function works

 This part explains every function in the same format, so anyone (developer, designer,
 investor or new team member) can understand how it works without you explaining it. Each
 function lists what it does, who gets it, where it is, how the user uses it, what happens
 behind the scenes, what is saved, what the user sees, special cases, what it never does, and
 the tests that prove it.

 Getting started
 Landing page and country check
 What it does             Explains Bloat and lets a visitor start; checks whether Bloat is available where they live.

 Who gets it              Everyone

 Where                    Screens 01, 02, 03

 How the user             1. Visitor opens the website and reads the headline, three steps, Free vs Pro and privacy
 uses it                  promise.
                          2. Taps Get started.
                          3. Chooses country: United States, Canada, India or Other.

 Behind the               • US, Canada, India: go to the help page (screen 04).
 scenes                   • Other: show "Bloat isn't available in your country yet" and an optional waitlist email.
                          • Prices are shown in the visitor's currency (USD default).

 What is saved            • Nothing for US/CA/IN until sign-up (country held in the open tab).
                          • Other: waitlist email and country only.

 What the user            "Track your bloating. Spot your possible triggers. Bring real data to your doctor."
 sees

 Special cases            • Visitor from another country with a VPN: they still choose their country; availability
                          follows the choice.

 Never                    • Asks health questions before sign-up.
                          • Uses ad trackers on the page.

 When to get medical help (help page)
 What it does             Shows everyone, before they start, which symptoms need emergency, same-day or
                          routine medical help.

 Who gets it              Everyone, free, always

 Where                    Screen 04; links on Log bloating, Other symptoms, Me, experiments, website footer

 How the user             1. New user reads the page during sign-up.
 uses it                  2. Taps I've read this.
                          3. Later, anyone can open it from any symptom screen.

 Behind the               • Text quotes the NHS bloating page word for word, with source and link; only the
 scenes                   emergency number is changed to the user's country: 911 (USA, Canada), 112 (India);
                          otherwise "your local emergency number".
                          • A stored copy of the NHS text is kept in the evidence file, and a test checks the app
                          matches it.
                          • Works without logging in.

 What is saved            • Date and time viewed, page version, acknowledged yes/no.

 What the user            Three sections: Call 911/112 now · Contact a doctor today or go to urgent care · Book a
 sees                     regular appointment. Source line: NHS, Bloating.

 Special cases            • User's language not supported: English page with a note; never machine-translated.

 Never                    • Asks questions about symptoms.
                          • Rewrites or adds to the NHS wording.
                          • Says "all clear" or "you're fine".
                          • Pauses or blocks anything.

 Warning-sign chips
 What it does             Lets anyone record a worrying symptom and immediately see the matching NHS advice at
                          the right level.

 Who gets it              Everyone, free

 Where                    Screen 16 ("Anything worrying with it?")

 How the user             1. While logging bloating, taps any of nine signs, or None.
 uses it

 Behind the               • Emergency level (call 911 in the US and Canada, 112 in India): sudden, severe stomach
 scenes                   ache with a swollen tummy · vomiting blood or vomit like ground coffee · severe difficulty
                          breathing.
                          • Urgent level (contact a doctor today or go to urgent care): vomiting · fever or feeling
                          hot, cold or shivery · swelling or lump in the tummy · can't pee, poo or pass gas.
                          • See a doctor level (book an appointment): blood in poo · weight loss without trying.
                          • Each shows the exact NHS sentence with its source; only service names and numbers
                          are changed for the country. Nothing is paused or blocked.
                          • Pro users who tick abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhoea or reflux in Other symptoms
                          also see the NHS sentence for it, once a day at most.

 What is saved            • Warning sign linked to the episode.

 What the user            "The NHS advises contacting a doctor today or going to urgent care if you have bloating
 sees                     with a swelling or lump in your tummy."

 Special cases            • Country without a listed number: "your local emergency number", never a guessed one.

 Never                    • Labels the situation itself.
                          • Gives an all-clear when no chip is ticked.
                          • Words the advice in its own way.

 AI ingredient check (typed)
 What it does             Lists only the ingredients in a dish that are often linked to gas or bloating, for the user to
                          confirm one by one.

 Who gets it              Pro

 Where                    Screens 20 → 21

 How the user             1. Types a dish, e.g. "chicken burrito with beans".
 uses it                  2. Sees: "Which of these did you have?" with a short list (e.g. black beans, onion, garlic,
                          sour cream, flour tortilla).
                          3. Each item is labelled "Seen in photo" or "Usually in this recipe" (typed dishes: always
                          "usually"). Marks each item Had / Didn't have / Not sure, or taps Had all of these / None of
                          these and changes any item.
                          4. Can add missing ones, or tap I don't know / Assume the usual recipe.
                          5. Saves.

 Behind the               • First use asks the AI consent tick.
 scenes                   • AI gets only the dish text and language; returns at most 20 items, each in one of 12 food
                          groups.
                          • An item left untouched is saved as Not sure, never as Didn't have. A Not sure item
                          makes only its own food group unknown for that day; the other groups still count.
                          • Groups the AI didn't list count as not present only for home-made meals or confirmed
                          saved dishes; for restaurant and other meals they are unknown, because the AI can miss
                          hidden ingredients.
                          • If AI fails twice, the meal saves with ingredients unknown.

 What is saved            • Each ingredient: answer (had / didnt_have / not_sure), where it came from, food group,
                          AI version.

 What the user            "These are foods often linked to gas or bloating (sources: NIH, NHS). AI suggestion, may
 sees                     be wrong. Mark each one."

 Special cases            • Restaurant sauce where the user isn't sure about garlic: garlic Not sure, everything else
                          as marked; the day still counts for the other groups.
                          • AI returns something odd or unsafe: rejected; manual entry still works.

 Never                    • Sends symptoms, health data or identity to AI.
                          • Turns an unmarked item into "didn't have".
                          • Says a food caused bloating.

 Tests                    Q45, Q95

 Possible triggers
 What it does             Shows which foods and habits are linked to the user's worse days, honestly and without
                          making chance look like a trigger.

 Who gets it              Pro

 Where                    Screen 30

 How the user             1. Opens Possible triggers; picks a date range.
 uses it

 Behind the               • Food window: a day's score is compared with food eaten that day plus meals from 6 pm
 scenes                   the day before, because bloating can come hours later. Meals eaten after the day's worst
                          bloating started don't count for that day.

                          own group. A food counts as not eaten only on All days with every meal known (for
