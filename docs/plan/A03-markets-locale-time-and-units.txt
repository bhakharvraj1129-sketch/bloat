 Time rules
  • Store every instant in UTC with IANA zone, offset, precision (exact / approximate / unknown),
    source and entered_at.
  • Each day uses the pinned analysis time zone. Changing it in Me shows a preview and takes effect
    at a stated boundary; history does not move.
  • Episodes crossing midnight keep one identity; duration is split per local date (23:00–02:00 = 1 h
    + 2 h). Daily worst comes from the review, not the episode peak.
  • Elapsed time uses UTC. Ambiguous clock times (DST) need an offset choice; non-existent times
    need correction. Future times and end-before-start are rejected.
  • Travel is logged as context (ACT-20); it never rewrites the analysis zone. Device clocks are never
    trusted for authorization.
  • Dates display in the user's format but are stored unambiguously (03/04 never flips meaning).
