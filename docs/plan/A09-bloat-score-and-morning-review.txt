 A09. Bloat Score and the morning review
 Bloat Score = the explicitly confirmed worst bloating for a completed day, 0–10. Today shows
 "Highest logged so far" instead. The old 0–100 peak × duration score is retired.

 Review rules
  • Appears on next open for yesterday only; older dates editable from History as late.
  • DY-02 is never pre-selected. A skip leaves fields unknown and never erases saved answers.
  • DY-03 answers: All / Mostly, missed a small snack or drink / Some / None recorded / Not sure / I
    ate and drank nothing. Mostly can show a food was present, never absent (absence needs All);
    labelled on results. [FIX 4.3.2]
  • 7-day average: last 7 completed dates; confirmed and late; at least 4 usable; label "Average of N
    days (M late)".
  • Target time 20–30 seconds, measured in beta.
