 E03. Glossary
 Term                                    Meaning

 Episode                                 One stretch of bloating, from I feel bloated to Bloating gone

 Bloat Score                             Confirmed worst bloating for a completed day, 0–10

 Highest logged so far                   Strongest reading entered today, before review

 Confirmed / Late / Provisional /        Day review states (A09)
 Conflict / Unknown

 Coverage                                Whether food, habits or bowel were fully recorded for a day

 Possible trigger                        A group or habit linked to worse days in the user's own records; never a
                                         cause

 Food group (TAG)                        One of 12 groups of foods often linked to gas or bloating

 Warning-sign chip                       A tap recording a worrying symptom that shows the matching help text

 Experiment                              A 7-day habit change or a 2-week food swap followed by a 1-week add-back

 FX / WB checks                          Swap-experiment eligibility questions / weekly wellbeing questions

 Personal Focus                          Deterministic cards adapting what each user sees

 Next best log                           Suggestion of the most useful thing to record next

 Keep an eye on it mode                  Lighter logging: daily score, up to 5 watched food groups, followed habits

 Recalled                                Ingredients added after upgrading for the last 7 days of free meals

 Worth watching                          A difference of 1 point or more that goes the same way in both halves of the
                                         date range

 General wellness                        A product supporting healthy habits without diagnosing or treating; not a
                                         regulated device

 Evidence file                           Sources, AI tests, changes and feedback records

 Idempotency key / revision              Duplicate-proof saves / version numbers on edits

 RLS                                     Database row-level security keeping each user's rows private
