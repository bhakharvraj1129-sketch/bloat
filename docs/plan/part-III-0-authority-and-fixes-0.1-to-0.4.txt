 spec, the v4.2 change record, earlier v4.3 PDFs) are history. Do not load their rules into a coding
 agent.

 0.1 v4.3.1 fixes: what changed and why
 These fixes came from following one user through six months of the v4.3 rules. Each rule
 was correct on its own; together they left too few usable days for the foods and habits

 people care about, and nothing to offer once the user had their answer. The founder's
 decision applies throughout: nothing is reviewed by anyone outside (no clinician,
 dietitian or lawyer). Where a review would normally be used, the most cautious behaviour is
 built instead.

 0.3 v4.4 gap-audit fixes
 A line-by-line check of the plan against clinical guidance (NHS, NIDDK, AGA 2023, NICE
 CG122, Mayo Clinic, Cangemi & Lacy 2022) found 36 gaps. The founder accepted all 36 on 7
 November 2026, with two decisions recorded below. These rules override earlier sections
 where they differ.

 0.4 v4.5: AI protection, Your journey and personal
 tracking
 Founder decisions of 8 November 2026: reinstate an AI spending limit with full protections;
 add a Your journey screen; make the experience personal in three ways; keep the decision
 of no outside review. These rules override earlier sections where they differ.

 AI protection (reverses the 7 November removal of the cap)
 Protection                   Rule

 Monthly spending limit       Configurable by the founder; default $50 during the beta. When reached, AI pauses
                              for all users until next month or until the limit is raised

 Budget alerts                Email to the founder at 50%, 80% and 100% of the monthly limit; no user data in the
                              email

 Per-user rate limit          At most 10 AI checks per user per hour

 Daily quota                  At most 40 AI checks per user per day (normal use is 3 to 6), reset at midnight in the
                              user's time zone

 Maximum request size         Dish text: the first 200 characters go to the AI (the full text is saved). Photos shrunk to
                              1600px and under 2 MB, otherwise not sent. At most 20 items back; maximum
                              answer length fixed in configuration

 Retry limit                  One retry after 2 seconds, then ingredients unknown; never automatic loops; the
                              same meal (same save key) is never sent twice

 Emergency kill switch        Feature flag ai_enabled turns all AI off within 1 minute, without a release

 Keys                         Server-side only; separate test and live keys; written steps to replace a key; the AI
                              provider's own spending limit as a second safety net

 Model setting                Only approved models; anything else refused at start-up with an alert. Alert when one
                              request costs 3 times the expected cost or more

 Meals always save            Whenever AI is paused, limited, failing or off: the meal saves with ingredients
                              unknown and the user can add them by hand. Messages: "The ingredient check is
                              paused right now. Your meal is saved." or "Your meal is saved. Add ingredients
                              yourself, or try the check again later."

 Your journey (Pro; replaces the Monthly summary, screen 29)
  • One screen with the user's whole story, so they never have to visit several screens: Since you
    started (days tracked, completeness, bloating days in the last 30, typical strength and episode
    length, a trend line); What your records show (patterns worth watching, Your cycle, medicine
    before/after); What you have tried (experiments and results); Your phase and what comes next;
    What is missing; the doctor line when due; a "Make doctor report" button that opens the doctor
    PDF with the same date range.
  • The Your usual card and the monthly sections move inside it; History links to it. Screens stay at
    39.
  • Sections are ordered by the user's goal (ON-16). Every figure carries its day count and the fixed
    line "This is a difference in your records, not a medical measure." where it compares.
  • Free users who tap it see the upgrade screen, with no preview of their findings.
