 Exercise (Pro)
 EX-01 type, EX-02 start, EX-03 duration, EX-04 intensity, EX-05 near a meal. Minutes from the last
 and to the next logged meal are calculated. Symptoms during or after come from episodes; no extra
 questions.

 Bowel (Pro)
 BW-01 occurrence and time, BW-02 seven text descriptions, BW-03 strain, BW-04 felt complete. An
 explicit "No" is different from no entry. Never labelled or alerted.

 Medicines (Free)
 MD-01 name, type, start date and precision; MD-02 stopped, dose changed, restarted with date.
 Record only; Bloat never comments on medicines.

 Health background (Pro)
 HC-01 conditions (14 options), HC-02 status and year, HC-03 lactose details, HC-04 tests with
 user-written results. Used only for: food-experiment eligibility, published exclusions, the doctor PDF.
 Family history, genetic data and ethnicity are never collected.
