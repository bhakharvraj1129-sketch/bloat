 Same price on the web, iPhone and Android in each country. No free trial: the Free plan never expires and acts as
 the trial. The price charged follows the payment method's country (Razorpay for Indian methods, the card's
 country on the web, the store account's country in the app stores), not the country picked at sign-up. Each longer
 plan costs less per month than the shorter one (India: ₹199, ₹183, ₹167 a month).

 Selling rules
  • Free logging never expires. Safety content is never paywalled.
  • Upgrade prompts state only true facts: "You have 14 scored days. Pro shows your chart and
    possible triggers." No fake teasers.
  • Yearly total shown first, then monthly equivalent; currency, tax presentation, renewal date and
    cancellation route before checkout.
  • Cancellation in two taps; never deletes data or past reports. Downgrade keeps records; Pro
    generation stops.
  • Offer record stores country, currency, amount in minor units, interval, trial terms, provider price
    ID and version; invoices never re-converted.
  • Payment provider webhook is authoritative; idempotent, ordered events; explicit states for failed,
    grace, cancelled, refunded, chargeback.
  • Free Today shows an honest progress counter: "14 days logged · 9 of 14 confirmed days needed
    for possible triggers". Real counts only.
  • Pause Pro for 1, 2 or 3 months sits next to Cancel: no charge, data kept, Pro screens locked until
    resume. The cancel screen offers "Download your doctor PDF first".
  • No free trial: the Free plan never expires and acts as the trial (Q210). Plans: monthly, 3 months,
    yearly; yearly shown first.
