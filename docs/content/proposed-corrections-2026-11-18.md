## 1. Kiwifruit experiment and card

### The source

British Dietetic Association, "Guidelines for the Dietary Management of Chronic Constipation in Adults". Neurogastroenterology and Motility 37(12), e70173, published online 13 October 2025. doi 10.1111/nmo.70173. Open access: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12623288/

The page itself shows a robot check to automated visitors, so the text was read through PubMed Central's own open-access record service for the same article.

What it says about kiwifruit (good practice statements):

> Consumption of 2–3 kiwifruit daily for at least 4 weeks may be recommended in constipation (evidence‐based recommendation).

> Kiwifruit without skin may be recommended in constipation (evidence‐based recommendation). While not assessed in studies, keeping the skin on will provide additional fiber, which may potentially be beneficial, but may also potentially increase side effects (expert opinion recommendation).

> Kiwifruit may be a preferred option over psyllium in people with constipation who experience side effects such as bloating, abdominal pain, and flatulence (evidence‐based recommendation)

Two more findings that matter for Bloat:

- Statement 24: kiwifruit raised stool frequency a little compared with psyllium (+0.36 bowel movements a week); the guideline calls the effect small. Evidence level for the kiwifruit statements: low to very low.
- Statement 22 (kiwifruit **supplements**, not fresh fruit): "reduce the frequency of abdominal pain in constipation, but not flatulence or bloating."
- The studies were in adults with **chronic constipation**.

### Compared with what Bloat has now

| | Bloat now | Guideline | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | 2 whole kiwifruit a day | 2–3 kiwifruit a day | Close. "Whole" suggests eating the skin; the guideline's evidence-based form is without skin. |
| Length | 7 days | At least 4 weeks | **7 days is too short** to match the guideline. |
| Who | Offered once constipation has been logged, if FX-03 (allergies) is No and no blocking condition | Adults with chronic constipation | One logged constipation day is a much lower bar than chronic constipation. |
| What it measures | The usual experiment result: bloating scores | Stool frequency and constipation symptoms | The guideline does not show kiwifruit easing bloating. Measuring bloating could mislead. |
| Source shown | "BDA 2025" (old link broken) | This guideline | Link needs replacing. |

### Proposed correction (for review, not applied)

- **Name:** Kiwifruit for regularity (unchanged).
- **Instruction:** "2 or 3 kiwifruit a day, peeled, for 4 weeks."
- **Length:** 28 days, not 7. This is longer than every other habit experiment, so the result screen would need a 4-week version of the template.
- **Who:** keep the allergy check (FX-03 No) and the blocking conditions. Offer it only after constipation was logged on several days. A suggested bar for the owner: constipation on at least 3 of the last 14 days. Not a diagnosis, and not decided by Bloat.
- **What it shows at the end:** bowel movements a week before and during, plus the person's own "did it help" answer. Do not present a bloating result as the main outcome. Keep the fixed wording that this is a personal record, not proof.
- **Source:** the guideline above, linked to PMC12623288.
- **Card text:** "Two or three kiwifruit a day, peeled, for at least 4 weeks may help with constipation." Label: "Some evidence". Source: the guideline above. Keep the existing FX-03 exclusion.

These touch an experiment's length, eligibility and result template, which are product rules. They need the owner's yes before any code changes.
