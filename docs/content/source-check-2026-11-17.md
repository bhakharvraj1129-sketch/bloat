## Help page ("When to get medical help")

The app shows exactly the text stored in the Master Plan. A test checks this.

But the plan's text is **not word for word** the NHS page, although the page says "quoted word for word". The meaning and every warning sign match today's NHS page. The wording is adapted, for example:

| NHS page today | App (from the plan) |
|---|---|
| Ask for an urgent GP appointment or call 111 if | Contact a doctor today or go to urgent care if |
| you cannot pee, poo or fart | you can't pee, poo or pass gas |
| you have a stomach ache that is severe and came on very suddenly | stomach pain that is severe and came on very suddenly |
| your vomit looks like ground coffee | your vomit looks like coffee grounds |
| you have bloating and unintentional weight loss | you have bloating and are losing weight without trying |

No warning sign is missing. This needs the owner's decision (DECISIONS.md item 11).
