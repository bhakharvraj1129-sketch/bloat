When to get medical help

Bloat can't tell you that you're fine. If you have bloating with any of these, don't wait for the app.
Call 911 now •Sudden, severe stomach ache with a swollen tummy•Vomiting blood, or vomit that looks like ground coffee•Severe difficulty breathing
Contact a doctor today or go to urgent care•Vomiting•Fever, or feeling hot, cold or shivery•Swelling or lump in my tummy•Can't pee, poo or pass gas
Book a doctor's appointment•Blood in poo•Weight loss without trying•Bloating on more than 12 of the last 30 days
