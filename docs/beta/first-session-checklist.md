# Bloat Buddy test version: your first session

About 15 minutes. **Made-up entries only.** If something doesn't match what is written after the arrow, note it on the feedback form.

1. Open **https://bloat-preview.vercel.app** and tap **Sign in** with the email address and temporary password you were given. → You land on Today.
2. **You, Change password:** choose a new one of at least 10 characters. → "Password changed. Use the new one next time you sign in."
3. **You, Plan.** → "Bloat Buddy Pro" and "Switched on for testing. There is no subscription, so nothing renews and nothing is charged." If it says "Bloat Buddy Free", tell the person who invited you.
4. Optional: add Bloat Buddy to your home screen and open it from there. → It opens on Today.
5. **Today, I feel bloated** (or the **+** button in the bottom bar): pick a made-up strength, Save. → "Bloating saved."
6. **Add to today, Meal:** type a made-up dish, Save. → "Meal saved."
7. **Add to today, Activity:** tick a habit, Save. → "Activity saved."
8. **History**, open today. → Your entries are there. Change the meal's time, then delete the meal. → Gone after a refresh.
9. Optional, to test food comparisons later: **You, Keep an eye on it**, choose 2 food groups.
10. **Tomorrow:** on Today, **Review yesterday**. → Yesterday gets its Bloat Score in History.
11. Send anything you noticed with the feedback form to the person who invited you. Then **Sign out** if you share the device.
