Thank you for helping test Bloat.

**What Bloat is.** A private diary for bloating: when it happens, how strong it is, what you ate, and your habits. Over time it shows patterns in your own records. It is a wellness tracker, not a medical service: it never diagnoses anything and never tells you what caused your bloating.

**This is a test version.** Things may change or break. Please keep your own copy of anything important: you can download all your records at any time (You, Download my data).

## Signing in

1. Open **https://bloat-preview.vercel.app** on your phone or computer.
2. Tap **Sign in** and use the email address and the temporary password you were given.
3. Then go to **You** and tap **Change password**. Choose a new password of at least 10 characters that you don't use anywhere else.

Forgot your password? Ask [ name of the person who invited you ] for a new temporary one, then change it again in You. Bloat sends no emails in this test version.

To add Bloat to your phone's home screen: in Chrome, open the menu and tap "Add to Home screen"; in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

## What you can use

- **Today:** "I feel bloated", with a strength from 1 to 10, when it started, and anything worrying with it. Update the strength, and end it when it's gone. "No bloating so far today" if it's a good day. Add meals by typing what you had. Add habits.
- **Morning review:** the next day, a few quick questions set that day's Bloat Score.
- **History:** every day, with Edit and Delete on each entry.
- **You:** units, time zone, habits to follow, medicines, download your data, change password, sign out, delete account.

*(If you have the extended version:)* **Patterns, reports and experiments:** possible triggers with day counts, a chart and 7-day average, a weekly report, Your journey, a doctor report you can share by link (and stop sharing at any time), small 7-day experiments, and bowel and exercise logs. Patterns are worked out from your own records by fixed rules; they show differences in your records, not causes.

## What is not in this test version

No photo or AI ingredient checks for meals (you type what you had), no Learn library or daily tips, no payments, and no emails from Bloat.

## Your health and your privacy

- If something worries you, open **When to get medical help** (on the Log bloating screen and in You). In an emergency, call your emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 112 in India).
- Your records are visible only to you.
- Anyone who has a doctor report link can open it, so send it only to the person it's for. It stops working when it expires, when you stop sharing it, or when you change or delete a record it shows. A downloaded copy can't be taken back.
- **Delete account** (in You) removes your account and every record for good.

## Telling us what you found

Write to [ contact email address ] with what you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private: a description of the screen is enough.
