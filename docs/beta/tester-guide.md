# Bloat test version: guide for invited testers

*For the owner, before sending: this guide is for the first invited acceptance round (3 to 5 trusted testers, Pro switched on for testing, made-up data only). Send it with the review address. Send each tester's temporary password separately (`owner-checklist.md`). **Reporting channel: awaiting the owner's selection.** Tell each tester which existing way to use (the one you already use with them); this guide only says "the person who invited you". There is nothing else to fill in.*

---

Thank you for helping test Bloat.

**What Bloat is.** A private diary for bloating: when it happens, how strong it is, what you ate, and your habits. Over time it shows patterns in your own records. It is a wellness tracker, not a medical service: it never diagnoses anything and never tells you what caused your bloating.

**This is a test version.** Things may change or break.

## Use made-up entries only

Please type **made-up entries only** during this round: invented times, strengths, meals and habits. Don't enter your real health details, medicines or conditions, even if they are true for you. The test is about whether the app works, not about your health.

## Signing in

1. Open **https://bloat-preview.vercel.app** on your phone (or a computer).
2. Tap **Sign in** and use the email address and temporary password you were given.
3. Go to **You** and tap **Change password**. Choose a new password of at least 10 characters that you don't use anywhere else.

Forgot your password? Ask the person who invited you for a new temporary one, then change it again in You. Bloat sends no emails in this test version.

To add Bloat to your home screen: in Chrome, open the menu and tap "Add to Home screen"; in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

## What you can use

Pro is switched on for your account for testing. There is no subscription: nothing to pay, renew, pause or cancel.

- **Today:** "I feel bloated" with a strength from 1 to 10, when it started and any warning signs; update the strength; end it. "No bloating so far today". Add a meal by typing what you had (when, how much, how fast, where it was made). Add habits. Log a bowel movement or exercise.
- **Morning review:** the next day, a few quick questions set that day's Bloat Score.
- **History:** every day, a chart (week, month, all) and a 7-day average. On a day: change times and a meal's name, and delete any entry, including a habit answer.
- **Patterns:** Possible triggers (with day counts), Your journey, the weekly report, and the doctor report, which you can download or share by link and stop sharing at any time.
- **You:** units, time zone, habits to follow, medicines, health background, family history, optional context, Keep an eye on it mode, hide charts, download your data (JSON or CSV), change password, sign out, delete account.
- **When to get medical help:** on the Log bloating screen and in You.

## What is not in this round

- No AI: no ingredient check or photos for meals, and no way to add ingredients or save a dish. Meals are saved as the text you type.
- No experiments, no Learn library and no daily cards.
- No payments, no emails from Bloat, no Google sign-in, and no new sign-ups.

## Good to know

- **Foods in Possible triggers.** Because meals have no ingredients in this round, the food groups in Possible triggers will say "No usable days yet" during normal logging. Habits you follow can be compared. To try the food comparisons, switch on **Keep an eye on it** in You: the morning review then asks yes or no for up to 5 food groups.
- Patterns need time: a food group or habit needs about 7 days with it and 7 days without.
- The doctor report has an "Experiments" section. In this round it says "None".
- A doctor report link opens for anyone who has it. It stops working when it expires, when you stop sharing it, or when you change or delete a record it shows.

## Your health and your privacy

- If something worries you, open **When to get medical help**. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 112 in India).
- Other testers cannot see your records. The person running the test can reach the database to look after it, which is one more reason to use made-up entries only.
- **Delete account** (in You) removes your account and every record for good.

## Telling us what you found

Use the feedback form (`feedback-template.md`) and send it to the person who invited you, in the way you agreed with them. One problem per message is easiest. A screenshot helps; check it shows only made-up entries.
